# Scolyra character artwork

Five approved designs use the existing whole-sprite idle, walk, attack, hit, and death animations. Combat stats and abilities are preserved. These clips do not articulate individual limbs or weapons.

Open Scene/ScolyraAnimationPreview.tscn and press F6, or F5 to play normally.

Created with built-in image generation. Shared prompt: full-body polished anime fantasy humanoid venomous arthropod warriors, centipede-inspired segmented armor, unique silhouettes and equipment, transparent background.

- Scyrix: crimson/charcoal fighter, jagged great cleaver.
- Mordrax: forest-green vanguard, venom gauntlets and plated tail.
- Veltrix: plum/bone marksman, organic repeating venom crossbow.
- Nyzara: midnight/violet flashstep assassin, linked chain blade.
- Thesira: ochre/aubergine support alchemist, injector bracers and venom vials.
