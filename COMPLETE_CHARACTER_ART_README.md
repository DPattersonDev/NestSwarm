Formicara and Lepidra complete all six factions. Each character now has artwork and the existing whole-sprite idle, walk, attack, hit, and death animations. These clips do not articulate individual limbs, wings or weapons. Combat balance and abilities are preserved.

Open Scene/AllCharactersAnimationPreview.tscn and press F6 for all 30 characters. Individual FormicaraAnimationPreview.tscn and LepidraAnimationPreview.tscn scenes are also available. F5 plays the game normally.
