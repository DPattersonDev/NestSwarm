# Caraphex character artwork

Five approved designs use the existing whole-sprite idle, walk, attack, hit, and death animations. Combat stats and abilities are preserved. These clips do not articulate individual limbs or weapons.

Open Scene/CaraphexAnimationPreview.tscn and press F6, or F5 to play normally.

Created with built-in image generation. Shared prompt: full-body polished anime fantasy humanoid beetle warriors, segmented chitin craftsmanship, distinct silhouettes and equipment, isolated transparent background.

- Brontis: broad older vanguard, indigo rhinoceros beetle armor, tower shield and ram gauntlet.
- Kharvos: rust/charcoal stag beetle fighter, two-handed seismic hammer.
- Ignivar: steel/orange bombardier beetle marksman, shoulder siege cannon.
- Virex: petrol blue jewel beetle assassin, piercing war pick and buckler.
- Aurelia: pearl/turquoise scarab support, shell-mending lantern and shell ward.

Assets: Art/Characters/Caraphex/. Validation: tests/caraphex_art_smoke.gd.
