# Anttalope character artwork

Five approved concepts use the existing whole-sprite idle, walk, attack, hit, and death animations. Stats and abilities are preserved. These clips do not articulate individual limbs or weapons.

Open Scene/AnttalopeAnimationPreview.tscn and press F6, or press F5 to play.

Created with built-in image generation. Shared prompt: full-body anime fantasy humanoid insect hunters, mantis-inspired armor, distinct silhouettes and equipment, transparent background.

- Kaelor: horned ivory/copper fighter, two curved swords.
- Droven: massive moss/stone vanguard, integrated mantis gauntlets.
- Nyxis: white/teal marksman, organic acid dart rifle and respirator.
- Syrra: aubergine/jade assassin, forearm scythe and grappling talon.
- Velkara: rose/bronze tactician, layered robes and floating crescents.

Assets: Art/Characters/Anttalope/. Test: tests/anttalope_art_smoke.gd.
