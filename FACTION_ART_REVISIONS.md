# Faction artwork revisions

24 character illustrations revised from approved previews. Existing texture paths, animations, stats and abilities are preserved. Six retained designs: Veyra, Melora, Brontis, Lunara, Noctren and Solvyr.

Faction palettes: Vesper gold/black/amber; Anttalope sage/olive/bone; Caraphex cobalt/graphite with iridescence; Scolyra plum/charcoal with venom green; Formicara oxblood/black/ivory. Lepidra retains varied wing colors and moon-silver details. Myraxa has a new silver-haired commander design, straight tabard and structured collar distinct from Karnyx.

Revised using built-in image generation with each previous illustration as the identity reference. Prompts specified individual armor, silhouette and faction palette changes while retaining recognizable equipment.

Test all characters in Scene/AllCharactersAnimationPreview.tscn with F6, or play with F5. Whole-sprite animation remains the current animation method.
